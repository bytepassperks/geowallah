AI SEO Agencies

An AI SEO agency gets your business named by AI tools and ranked by search engines. Here is what they really do and how to pick a good one.

By Sayan Roy Chowdhury · 2026-07-19 · 5 min read

What does an AI SEO agency actually do?

An AI SEO agency gets your business named by AI tools and ranked by search engines, by fixing the signals both rely on. The good ones do not sell magic. They audit your facts, rebuild your Google presence, shape your content into quotable answers, and earn the reviews and mentions that make a model trust you. The AI part is the goal, not a gimmick bolted onto old tactics.

Plenty of agencies have simply added "AI" to the same tired pitch and changed nothing else. Telling the real ones apart from the rebrands is the whole point of this guide, and it is easier than it sounds once you know what to listen for.

How is an AI SEO agency different from a regular one?

A regular SEO agency aims for a ranking position. An AI SEO agency aims for that and for the spoken answer.

The difference shows up in what they measure. A traditional agency reports rankings and traffic. An AI-focused one also checks whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google's AI box name you for your key questions, then works to close the gaps. If an agency cannot tell you where you stand in those answers today, it is not really doing AI SEO, whatever the slide deck says.

What tools do these agencies use?

Tools help, but they do not replace judgement. A capable agency uses a mix and never leans on any single one.

  • Research tools like Ahrefs, Semrush, or the free Google Search Console for keywords and questions.
  • Technical crawlers such as Screaming Frog to find the faults that quietly sink a site.
  • Schema and validation tools to mark up content and check it reads correctly.
  • AI answer tracking, often a simple, disciplined habit of querying the assistants directly and logging who gets named.

Be wary of anyone selling a single "AI SEO tool" as the answer. The tools surface problems. The skill is knowing which ones to fix first.

How do you choose a good AI SEO agency?

  1. Ask for proof. A business they rank, the query, and ideally an AI answer that names that client.
  2. Check the method. They should explain, in plain words, how they earn a mention rather than guard a secret.
  3. Demand reporting. Monthly visibility on rankings, traffic, and AI mentions, not vague reassurance.
  4. Watch for guarantees. Nobody controls these systems, so a promised number one is a red flag.
  5. Mind the fit. A small local business and a national store need different plans, so one flat package suits neither well.

What does honest AI SEO work look like?

It looks unglamorous and it works. At GEOwallah, the approach for a local client is to make the facts agree, strengthen the Google profile, write pages a machine can quote, and earn real reviews. That method took Emily's Pet Heaven in Barrackpore from invisible to the name both Google and ChatGPT give for the best pet boarding nearby. No tricks, no link farms, just the signals done right and in the right order.

What should you ask on the first call?

Treat the first call as an audition. A few blunt questions reveal more than any glossy proposal.

Ask where your business is named today by ChatGPT and Gemini, and whether they checked before the call. Ask for one client they helped and the exact query that proves it. Ask what they would fix first, and why that order. Ask how they report progress, and how often you will see it. A strong agency answers plainly and shows its working. A weak one retreats into jargon, dodges the proof, or promises a number it cannot control. The call should feel like a clear conversation, not a sales pitch you have to decode line by line.

What does the wrong agency cost you?

More than its fee. A weak agency burns months on busywork while a rival quietly becomes the AI answer in your category. Worse, an agency chasing shortcuts, bulk links or thin auto-spun pages, can earn you a penalty that takes far longer to undo than it took to cause. The real price of the wrong choice is the head start you hand your competition. That is why the dull questions, about proof, method, and reporting, matter more than any polished pitch.

Key takeaways

  • A real AI SEO agency measures whether assistants name you, not just rankings and traffic.
  • Tools surface problems; the skill is knowing which to fix first, so beware any single 'AI SEO tool' sold as the answer.
  • Choose on proof, method, and reporting, and treat guaranteed number-one rankings as a red flag.
